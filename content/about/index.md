My full name is Gabriel Isaac Alonso Serrato, and I also make things as *Arthifact*. My background brings together computer science and environmental engineering; my research direction is geometry processing.

I’m interested in how geometric representations connect to simulation and computer graphics. During the Summer Geometry Initiative in 2025, I explored differentiable meshing and co-authored an [essay on DMesh++](https://summergeometry.org/sgi2025/dmesh/).

Alongside research, I build interactive systems and open-source tools, and create 3D art and animation. This site collects that work, together with the notes I make along the way.
