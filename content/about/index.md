I’m a graduate student in environmental engineering working on inverse problems. Basically, that means starting with the data and working backwards to figure out what caused it.

My interests are in data representation and geometry processing. The idea is that finding the right shape for the data can make a problem a little easier to solve. That’s good news if, like me, you’d rather not wrestle with ridiculously complicated algorithms. Plus, our brains like patterns, so we might as well give them something to work with :)

Outside of research, I love making video games and interactive apps. Lately, that’s meant exploring better tools for content creation, ideally ones that are as fun to use as they are to make.

Anyway, take a look around. My research and projects are here :)
