Hi! I’m Isaac, I’m a computer scientist, I graduated from The University of Texas at El Paso (UTEP) in December 2025. Right now I’m working on a few research projects at UTEP while I apply to grad school.

I’ve always loved movies, and the way motion can tell a story. That’s what pulled me into computer graphics: animation, games, procedural worlds, anything that moves or grows from a few simple rules. Nature does this better than anyone, which is why I love it so much. My dream is to make games and tools that help people build better digital worlds.

In research, I work on inverse problems, starting from the data and working backwards to find what caused it. I’ve been giving that data a better shape with Gaussians, meshes and voxels, from seismic imaging to 3D reconstruction. [See my research →](/research/)

I also believe in active learning. In my last year and a half at UTEP, I started [ArTecs](https://www.instagram.com/artecs.utep/), a student organization that teaches creative coding and game development. Now I’m founding Curiosity Spark to make education more interactive and fun, starting with our first project, [Slidekick](https://arthifact.com/slidekick/). [See my projects →](/projects/)

And when I’m not coding, I’m drawing, modeling and animating in Procreate, Blender and MagicaVoxel. [See my art →](/art/)
