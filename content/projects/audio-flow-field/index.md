An audio-reactive particle flow field. Thousands of particles drift through a noise field, and the music bends it: the bass speeds the flow up and pushes it outward on every hit, the mids steer it sideways, and the treble lifts it. Louder passages bring more particles and brighter trails, and quiet recordings are lifted for the analysis so they still move. Drop in any track and it runs in the browser. This demo plays Taro by alt-J.
