Slidekick turns your slides into an interactive lesson. Drop in a PDF from PowerPoint, Google Slides, Keynote or Canva, and a guide explains each slide in speech bubbles, with questions in between: multiple choice, drag and drop, and mix and match. The whole lesson exports as a single HTML file you can email, upload to Google Classroom or open offline. It is the first project of Curiosity Spark.
