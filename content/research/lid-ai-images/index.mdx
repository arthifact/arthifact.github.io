## Problem

Generated images are getting hard to tell apart from real ones. Instead of looking at the pixels, we looked at where each image lands inside a neural network's feature space, where real and generated images might be spread out differently. So the goal was to test if a single geometric measure could tell them apart.

## How it works

My hypothesis was that real images sit in richer neighbourhoods than generated ones. To test it:

- **Features:** 100,000 CIFAR-10-sized images, half real and half generated, passed through a ResNet-18.
- **LID:** for each image, local intrinsic dimensionality estimates how many directions the data really spreads in around it, from the distances to its nearest neighbours.
- **Classifier:** one threshold on LID, with the number of neighbours k and the cutoff tuned together.
