From June to August 2023 I worked at the Center of Nanoscience and Nanotechnology (CNyN) of UNAM with Jonathan Guerrero on the molecular geometry of molybdenum disulfide (MoS₂). This was an introductory project and my first time doing research, so the goal was to develop a small tool to search many possible structures and pick a few worth simulating.

## Problem

MoS₂ is a layered material, and its atoms can be arranged over a lattice in many ways. Each full simulation is expensive, so the goal was an exploration tool that works with any MoS₂ lattice and narrows the candidates down before the heavy computation.

## How it works

The tool was written in Python with NumPy and Matplotlib, is a brute-force search over the lattice:

- **Lattice:** start from a lattice of molybdenum and sulfur atoms.
- **Try every combination:** go through groups of sulfur atoms and build a triangle or a hexagon around each group's centroid.
- **Snap to atoms:** move each corner to the nearest real atom, and keep only the shapes that still fit the lattice.
- **Save:** plot every kept shape and export its coordinates.
